Stage 1 – Team Formation and Idea Development
In Stage 1, the team focused on building a collaborative and organized working environment while exploring potential project ideas for the portfolio project.

The team held initial meetings to introduce members, discuss strengths and interests, assign temporary roles, and establish communication and collaboration strategies using tools such as Discord, GitHub Projects, Notion, and Figma.

Several project ideas were brainstormed and evaluated based on:

Feasibility
Technical alignment
Potential impact
Scalability
Market need

The team used brainstorming techniques such as Mind Mapping, SCAMPER, and “How Might We” questions to generate and refine ideas.

After evaluating multiple ideas, the team selected the Sports Field Booking Platform as the final MVP idea. It is a digital platform designed to help users find nearby sports fields, view their information, and book them through one easy-to-use platform.

Main MVP Features
Search for sports fields based on the user's location.
Display nearby sports fields and their information.
View field details, including the sport type and available facilities.
Book sports fields.
Women's Feature to help users identify sports fields that provide suitable options for women.
Challenges Identified

Collecting accurate and up-to-date information about sports fields.
Maintaining accurate field availability schedules.
Implementing location-based functionality effectively.
Designing a clear and easy booking process.
Delivering the MVP within the project timeline.

Opportunities
Support different types of sports and facilities.
Add maps and filters based on sport, price, distance, and availability.
Add ratings, reviews, and online payment in the future.
Expand the women's feature by providing more information about suitable facilities for women.
Expand the platform to include more sports fields and locations in the future.
