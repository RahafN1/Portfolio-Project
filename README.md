# Stage 1 – Team Formation and Idea Development

## Overview

In Stage 1, the team focused on building a collaborative and organized working environment while exploring potential ideas for the portfolio project.

The team held initial meetings to introduce members, discuss strengths and interests, assign temporary roles, and establish communication and collaboration strategies using tools such as **Discord, GitHub Projects, Notion, and Figma**.

---

## Idea Evaluation

Several project ideas were brainstormed and evaluated based on the following criteria:

* **Feasibility**
* **Technical Alignment**
* **Potential Impact**
* **Scalability**
* **Market Need**

The team used brainstorming techniques such as:

* **Mind Mapping**
* **SCAMPER**
* **How Might We Questions**

These techniques helped the team generate, discuss, and refine different project ideas.

---

## Selected Idea

After evaluating multiple ideas, the team selected the **Sports Field Booking Platform** as the final **MVP** idea.

The platform is designed to help users **find nearby sports fields, view their information, and book them through one easy-to-use platform**.

### Main MVP Features

* Search for sports fields based on the user's location.
* Display nearby sports fields and their information.
* View field details, including the sport type and available facilities.
* Book sports fields.
* **Women's Feature** to help users identify sports fields that provide suitable options for women.

---

## Challenges Identified

The team identified the following challenges:

* Collecting accurate and up-to-date information about sports fields.
* Maintaining accurate field availability schedules.
* Implementing location-based functionality effectively.
* Designing a clear and easy booking process.
* Delivering the MVP within the project timeline.

---

## Opportunities

The platform can be expanded in the future through:

* Supporting different types of sports and facilities.
* Adding maps and filters based on **sport, price, distance, and availability**.
* Adding **ratings, reviews, and online payment**.
* Expanding the **Women's Feature** by providing more information about suitable facilities for women.
* Expanding the platform to include more sports fields and locations.
